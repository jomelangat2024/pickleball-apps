Singles Flex League

Players are grouped into flights by level for standings. Each week you create the matchups by hand — pick any two players (even across flights), and the same pair can play again in a later week. It's flex: you and your opponent coordinate your own court time and record the result by the Sunday night deadline. Match format: best 2 of 3 games to 11, win by 2. Standings: win % → game differential → point differential → fewest points allowed. Top finishers move up a flight next season; bottom finishers move down.
